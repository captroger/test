Best Online Writing Courses in 2026 — Learn From the Best

Master fiction, nonfiction, copywriting, and creative writing with courses from bestselling authors and top platforms.

By TextTally Team | Published: August 14, 2026 · 7 min read

Great writers are not born — they are made through deliberate practice, quality feedback, and structured learning. Online writing courses give you access to instructors and techniques that were once only available in expensive MFA programs. Whether you want to write a novel, launch a blog, or break into copywriting, there is a course for you. Here are the best online writing courses and platforms of 2026.

1. MasterClass — Learn Writing From Icons

MasterClass offers the most star-studded writing courses on the internet. Learn fiction from Margaret Atwood (The Handmaid's Tale), storytelling from Neil Gaiman (American Gods), thriller writing from Dan Brown (The Da Vinci Code), creative nonfiction from Malcolm Gladwell, and screenwriting from Aaron Sorkin (The Social Network).

Each course includes 15–20 video lessons (typically 10–20 minutes each), a downloadable workbook with exercises, and access to a community discussion board. The production quality is cinematic — these are not talking-head lectures.

Best courses for writers:

  • Margaret Atwood Teaches Creative Writing — Covers plot, character, point of view, and the writing process.
  • Neil Gaiman Teaches the Art of Storytelling — How to find ideas, build worlds, and develop your unique voice.
  • Malcolm Gladwell Teaches Writing — Research techniques, narrative structure, and crafting compelling nonfiction.
  • Roxane Gay Teaches Writing for Social Change — How to write with purpose and conviction about issues that matter.

Pricing: $10/month (billed annually at $120) for full access to all 180+ courses across all categories. A single month at $15 is also available.

2. Skillshare — Best for Project-Based Learning

Skillshare takes a different approach: every class includes a hands-on project you complete and share with the community. Writing classes typically run 30–90 minutes, broken into 5–10 minute lessons — perfect for fitting into a busy schedule. The platform has hundreds of writing courses covering fiction, nonfiction, copywriting, screenwriting, poetry, and journaling.

Notable writing courses:

  • Creative Writing for All: A 10-Day Journaling Challenge by Emily Gould — Build a daily writing habit.
  • Copywriting for Beginners: How to Write Web Copy That Sells — Practical, conversion-focused copywriting.
  • Storytelling 101: Character, Conflict, Context & Craft by Daniel José Older — The fundamentals of fiction.

Pricing: $13.99/month or $99/year. Skillshare offers a generous 30-day free trial, giving you a full month to explore multiple courses before committing.

3. Coursera — University-Level Writing Courses, Free to Audit

Coursera partners with top universities to offer structured, semester-style courses. The big advantage: you can audit most courses for free — watching all lectures and accessing readings without paying. Paid certificates ($49/month subscription) add graded assignments, peer feedback, and a shareable credential.

Recommended writing courses:

  • Creative Writing Specialization (Wesleyan University) — A 5-course series covering plot, character, setting, and style. The capstone project is a complete short story, novella, or memoir excerpt.
  • Good with Words: Writing and Editing Specialization (University of Michigan) — Focuses on professional writing and editing skills with practical exercises.
  • Memoir and Personal Essay: Write About Yourself (Wesleyan) — How to turn personal experiences into compelling narratives.

Pricing: Free to audit each course. $49/month for the Coursera Plus subscription (includes certificates and graded assignments).

4. Udemy — Best for One-Off Course Purchases

Udemy operates on a per-course purchase model, with courses often discounted to $15–$25 during sales (which happen frequently). Courses are self-paced with lifetime access. The quality varies more than MasterClass or Coursera — check ratings, reviews, and preview videos before buying.

Top-rated writing courses on Udemy:

  • Writing With Flair: How to Become an Exceptional Writer (4.5 stars, 100K+ students) by Shani Raja, former Wall Street Journal editor.
  • The Complete Copywriting Course: Write to Sell Like a Pro (4.5 stars) — Covers web copy, email campaigns, ads, and landing pages.
  • Novel Writing 101: Turn Ideas into Captivating Stories (4.4 stars) — Step-by-step from concept to completed first draft.

5. Copyhackers — Best for Professional Copywriters

Copyhackers is the gold standard for conversion copywriting education. Their courses — Copy School and 10x Emails — teach the psychology and strategy behind writing that drives sales, sign-ups, and conversions. These are not beginner hobby classes; they are serious investments for writers who want to make copywriting their career.

Pricing: Courses range from $297 to $1,997. The price reflects the depth: Copy School includes 8+ hours of video, swipe files, templates, and direct feedback from professional copywriters. Not cheap, but the ROI for working copywriters is significant.

Which Writing Course Platform Is Right for You?

  • Want to learn from the world's best authors? MasterClass — there is nothing else like it for star power and production quality.
  • Prefer hands-on, project-based learning on a budget? Skillshare with its 30-day free trial — complete a class every few days.
  • Want a university-level creative writing credential? Coursera's Wesleyan Specialization — free to audit, structured like a real course.
  • Want a specific skill like copywriting or blogging? Udemy — buy one course and own it forever.
  • Ready to make copywriting your career? Copyhackers — the investment pays for itself with better-paying clients.
