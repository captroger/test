Writing Tips for Bloggers: How to Keep Readers Engaged

Practical advice for creating blog content that readers love and share.

By TextTally Team | Published: July 2026

Blogging is one of the most effective ways to share your knowledge, build an audience, and even generate income. But with millions of blog posts published every day, how do you make yours stand out? This guide shares proven writing tips for bloggers who want to keep readers engaged.

1. Write Compelling Headlines

Your headline is the first thing readers see. Use numbers, power words, and clear benefits. "10 Tips to Improve Your Writing" outperforms "Writing Tips." Keep headlines under 60 characters for SEO. Use TextTally's character counter to check your headline length.

2. Hook Readers in the First Paragraph

The first 50 words determine whether readers continue. Start with a surprising fact, a question, or a relatable problem. Avoid long introductions. Get to the point quickly.

3. Use Short Paragraphs and Sentences

Online readers scan rather than read. Break your content into short paragraphs (2-3 sentences max). Use bullet points, numbered lists, and subheadings to make your content scannable. Use TextTally's word counter to track your post length.

4. Write for Your Audience

Know who you are writing for and address their pain points. Use language your audience understands. Avoid jargon unless your readers are experts. Write conversationally, as if you were speaking to a friend.

5. Add Internal Links

Internal links keep readers on your site longer and improve SEO. Link to your other relevant blog posts and pages. For example, link to your about page or tools collection.

6. Optimize for Reading Time

Use TextTally's reading time calculator to check how long your post takes to read. Most readers prefer posts that take 5-10 minutes to read (1,000-2,000 words). Display reading time at the top of your posts to set expectations.

7. End with a Call to Action

Every blog post should end with a clear next step. Ask readers to leave a comment, share the post, sign up for your newsletter, or check out a related post. Engagement signals like comments and shares boost your SEO.

Final Thoughts

Blogging success comes from consistent, high-quality content. Use these tips to improve your writing, and use TextTally to track your word count, reading time, and character counts. For more resources, read our word counter guide.
