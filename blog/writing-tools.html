Best Free Writing Tools for Students in 2026

Essential tools every student needs for essays, assignments, and research papers.

By TextTally Team | Published: July 2026

Being a student in 2026 means having access to incredible free writing tools that can dramatically improve your essays, research papers, and assignments. In this guide, we have compiled the best free writing tools every student should know about.

1. TextTally — Word & Character Counter

Our very own TextTally is the cleanest, fastest word counter available. It provides instant statistics for words, characters (with and without spaces), sentences, paragraphs, reading time, and speaking time. It works completely offline, respects your privacy by processing everything in your browser, and offers multiple themes for comfortable writing. Best of all, it is completely free with no sign-up required.

2. Grammarly — Grammar and Spelling Checker

Grammarly is the industry standard for grammar checking. The free version catches spelling mistakes, punctuation errors, and basic grammar issues. It integrates with your browser and works across websites, email, and documents. For students, Grammarly is invaluable for catching errors before submission.

3. Hemingway Editor — Readability Improver

The Hemingway Editor helps you write clear, concise prose. It highlights complex sentences, passive voice, and adverbs. The free web version is excellent for editing essays and making your writing more readable. It pairs perfectly with TextTally's reading time calculator to ensure your content is accessible.

4. Google Docs — Collaborative Writing

Google Docs remains the best free collaborative writing tool. It offers real-time collaboration, version history, voice typing, and integration with research tools. Combined with TextTally for word counting, it is a complete writing solution for group projects.

5. Zotero — Citation Management

Zotero is a free, open-source citation manager that helps students collect, organize, and cite research sources. It automatically generates bibliographies in MLA, APA, Chicago, and thousands of other styles.

6. LibreOffice — Free Word Processor

LibreOffice is a powerful, free alternative to Microsoft Word. It handles all major document formats and includes advanced features like mail merge, table of contents generation, and track changes.

7. FocusWriter — Distraction-Free Writing

FocusWriter provides a minimalist writing environment that hides all distractions. It features a clean interface, daily goals, and typewriter sound effects. Use it with TextTally to check your word count as you write.

8. Canva — Visual Content Creation

Canva is a free graphic design tool for creating presentations, infographics, posters, and social media graphics. The free version includes thousands of templates suitable for academic projects.

Putting It All Together

With these free tools, you have everything you need to write better essays, research papers, and assignments. Start with TextTally's word counter to track your progress, use Grammarly for proofreading, and Hemingway for readability. For more resources, check out our tools page and word counter guide.
