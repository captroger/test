How TextTally Works

The clean, minimal, blazing-fast word and character counter.

How the Word Counter Works

TextTally is a pure client-side application. When you type or paste text into the text area, our JavaScript code immediately analyzes it in your browser. Here is exactly how each statistic is calculated:

  • Words: The text is split by whitespace, and non-empty segments are counted as words.
  • Characters (with spaces): The total length of the text string including spaces and punctuation.
  • Characters (no spaces): The total length of the text string with all whitespace characters removed.
  • Sentences: Counted by splitting on sentence-ending punctuation (. ! ?).
  • Paragraphs: Counted by splitting on double newline characters.
  • Reading Time: Word count divided by 200 WPM (average adult reading speed).
  • Speaking Time: Word count divided by 130 WPM (average conversational speaking speed).

Privacy-First Architecture

TextTally is designed to be completely private. Unlike online tools that send your text to servers for processing, TextTally runs entirely in your browser. This means:

  • Your text never leaves your device
  • No data is stored on our servers
  • No cookies track your writing
  • The tool works offline after the first load
  • You can clear your saved text at any time

For more details, read our privacy policy.

Features
