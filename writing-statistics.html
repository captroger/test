Average Word Counts by Genre

Word count expectations vary significantly by genre and format. Whether you're writing a novel, short story, essay, or blog post, knowing the typical length helps you plan, edit, and meet publisher or platform requirements.

Format / GenreTypical Word CountNotes
Literary Fiction Novel80,000 – 100,000Most debut novels fall in the 80k–90k range.
Science Fiction / Fantasy Novel90,000 – 120,000World-building often requires more words. Epics can exceed 150k.
Romance Novel50,000 – 90,000Category romance (Harlequin) is typically 50k–55k.
Young Adult (YA) Novel50,000 – 80,000YA fantasy can extend to 90k; contemporary YA is shorter.
Middle Grade Novel25,000 – 50,000Upper MG can reach 60k for fantasy.
Novella17,500 – 40,000Longer than a short story, shorter than a novel.
Short Story1,000 – 7,500Flash fiction is under 1,000 words.
College Essay (Undergraduate)1,500 – 5,000Varies by course and assignment type.
PhD Thesis / Dissertation40,000 – 80,000STEM theses tend to be shorter than humanities.
Blog Post (SEO-Optimized)1,500 – 2,500Top-ranking Google results average ~1,447 words.
LinkedIn Article500 – 1,500Long-form posts perform best; maximum is ~125,000 characters.
Press Release300 – 500One page is the industry standard; keep it concise.

Sources: Writer's Digest, Science Fiction & Fantasy Writers of America, HubSpot (2025 data), academic publisher guidelines.

Interesting Writing Facts & Figures

  • Longest novel ever published: Artamène ou le Grand Cyrus by Madeleine de Scudéry — approximately 1,954,300 words across 13,095 pages.
  • Fastest typist: The Guinness World Record for fastest typing is 216 words per minute on a keyboard (Stella Pajunas, 1946, on an IBM electric typewriter).
  • Most translated book: The Bible has been translated into over 700 languages. Among secular works, The Little Prince leads with translations into 300+ languages.
  • Average email length: The average business email is approximately 75–100 words. Emails with 50–125 words have the highest response rates (~50%).
  • National Novel Writing Month (NaNoWriMo): Participants aim to write 50,000 words in 30 days — approximately 1,667 words per day.
  • Average book sales: The average traditionally published book sells about 3,000 copies in its lifetime. Self-published books average around 250 copies.
  • Words in the English language: The Oxford English Dictionary contains over 600,000 entries. The average native English speaker knows 20,000–35,000 words.
  • Most common words: "The" is the most common word in English, accounting for approximately 7% of all words in typical text.
  • Optimal blog post frequency: Companies that publish 16+ blog posts per month generate 3.5x more traffic than those publishing 0–4 posts (HubSpot, 2025).

Sources: Guinness World Records, Guinness World Records typing records, Wycliffe Global Alliance, Boomerang email study, NaNoWriMo, Oxford English Dictionary, HubSpot marketing statistics (2025).
