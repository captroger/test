Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about TextTally.

General Questions

Is TextTally free to use?

Yes, TextTally is completely free. There are no hidden fees, premium tiers, or subscription requirements. All features are available to everyone.

Does TextTally work offline?

Yes, after the first visit, TextTally works completely offline. All calculations are performed in your browser with no server communication.

Is my text private?

Absolutely. Your text never leaves your device. TextTally processes everything in your browser. We do not store, log, or transmit your text anywhere. See our privacy policy for details.

Do I need to create an account?

No account or sign-up is required. TextTally is ready to use immediately.

Features

How does the word counter work?

TextTally uses JavaScript to analyze your text in real-time. It splits text by whitespace to count words, counts characters directly, and uses standard formulas for reading and speaking time.

What reading speed does TextTally use?

TextTally uses 200 WPM for reading time and 130 WPM for speaking time, which are industry-standard benchmarks. For more details, see our reading time calculator.

What themes are available?

TextTally offers five themes: Light, Dark, Sepia, Forest, and Ocean. You can switch between them using the theme selector in the header.

Does TextTally have a grammar checker?

Yes, TextTally includes a built-in client-side grammar checker that scans for common issues directly in your browser. See our grammar tips for more information.

How do I save my text?

Click the Save button or press Ctrl+S (Cmd+S on Mac) to save your text to your browser's LocalStorage.

Usage

What is the difference between word count and character count?

Word count counts complete words. Character count counts individual letters, numbers, punctuation, and spaces. Both are useful for different purposes. Our character counter page explains more.

Can I use TextTally for languages other than English?

Yes, TextTally works with any language that uses spaces between words. For languages like Chinese or Japanese, character count is more relevant.

Is there a word limit?

There is no practical word limit. The tool can handle very long texts.
