What Is a Character Counter?

A character counter is a simple but essential tool that counts every letter, number, punctuation mark, and space in your text. Whether you are composing a tweet, writing a LinkedIn post, drafting an SMS message, or optimizing a meta description, knowing your exact character count helps you stay within platform limits.

TextTally's character counter gives you instant results with two key metrics: characters with spaces and characters without spaces. This dual view is important because different platforms count characters differently — some include spaces, others do not.

For full text statistics including word count, sentence count, and reading time, use our main word counter tool.

Why Character Limits Matter

Social media platforms, messaging services, and SEO tools all enforce strict character limits. Exceeding them means your content gets cut off, rejected, or doesn't display properly. Here are the most common limits:

  • X (Twitter): 280 characters per tweet (up to 4,000 for Premium subscribers)
  • LinkedIn: 3,000 characters for posts; 150 characters for article summaries
  • SMS: 160 characters per message (longer texts split into 153-character segments)
  • Google Meta Descriptions: Typically 150–160 characters displayed in search results
  • Facebook Posts: 63,206 characters maximum (though engagement drops with longer posts)

Using a character counter before you post saves you from embarrassing truncations and ensures your message lands exactly as intended.

Frequently Asked Questions

How to count characters online?

Simply paste or type your text into TextTally's word counter at texttally.nl. The tool instantly displays the total character count with and without spaces alongside other useful statistics.

What is the Twitter character limit?

X (formerly Twitter) has a character limit of 280 characters for standard tweets. X Premium subscribers can write up to 4,000 characters per post.

What is the LinkedIn character limit?

LinkedIn posts are limited to 3,000 characters. LinkedIn article descriptions are capped at 150 characters for the summary field that appears in feeds.

Do character counts include spaces?

Most character counters, including TextTally, show both counts: characters with spaces and characters without spaces. Different platforms count differently, so checking both metrics is good practice.

Is a character counter the same as a word counter?

A character counter counts individual letters, numbers, punctuation, and spaces. A word counter counts complete words. TextTally's word and character counter does both simultaneously.
