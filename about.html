About TextTally

The clean, minimal, blazing-fast word and character counter.

Our Mission

TextTally was founded with a single mission: to create the cleanest, fastest, and most focused text statistics tool on the web. We believe that professional utilities should not be cluttered with intrusive ads, heavy analytics, or pop-ups requesting email registrations. Whether you are a student editing an essay, a novelist counting chapters, a marketer crafting copy, or a publisher formatting summaries, TextTally gives you instant feedback when you need it.

Our vision is to become the most trusted word counter on the web — a tool that loads instantly, respects your privacy, and works everywhere. We are committed to keeping TextTally free, fast, and ad-light.

Our Values

  • Privacy First: Your text never leaves your device. All processing happens in your browser, not on our servers. This is a fundamental design principle, not an afterthought.
  • Zero Friction: No sign-ups, no accounts, no email required. Open the tool and start typing immediately.
  • Performance: Every millisecond counts. TextTally uses minimal, optimized code to deliver instant results.
  • Accessibility: Our tool works with screen readers, supports keyboard navigation, and follows WCAG guidelines.
  • Transparency: We are open about how our tool works, what data we collect, and how we use it. Read our privacy policy and terms of service.

🔒 Privacy-First Guarantee

Your writing is personal, confidential, and valuable. We respect that. TextTally is designed to be an offline-first, client-side utility:

  • Zero Server Uploads: Every calculation, text count, paragraph analysis, and grammar check is performed entirely in your web browser.
  • Local Persistence: Using the manual Save button stores your text inside your own device's LocalStorage.
  • No Tracking: We do not track your typing habits, keep copy logs, or construct user profiles.

Core Features

  • Blazing-Fast Calculations: Instantly displays counts for words, characters, sentences, paragraphs, and read/speak durations.
  • Responsive Design: Adapts fluidly across mobile, tablet, and desktop screens.
  • Built-in Grammar Engine: Scans syntax and spelling directly in your browser using client-side rules.
  • Customizable Themes: Five themes including Light, Dark, Sepia, Forest, and Ocean.
  • Offline Capable: Works without an internet connection after the first load.
  • Multiple Text Sizes: Choose from Small, Medium, or Large text for comfortable reading.
